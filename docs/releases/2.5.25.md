## Highlights

- **Codex keeps native MCP results.** Structured content appears beside text, persisted history cards restore their real arguments and output instead of a completion placeholder, and code-mode patch scripts join their `patch +=` fragments correctly.
- **Pi history recovers more.** Local sessions are found under the package-selected config directory, and terminal errors are restored on the assistant message that failed.
- **Custom answers survive.** Grok and DeepSeek questions accept typed “Other” answers, and Grok returns them through native annotations and message parsing.
- **Stop hook feedback stays visible.** Blocking Stop hooks render as their own block inside the turn, both live and when replaying native transcripts, and the reply continues afterward.
- **Editing and composing are steadier.** Inline edits apply the mode’s result regardless of reply wrapper, a consumed Escape stays inside its suggestion menu, and moving the caret out of an @ query closes the mention menu.
- **Settings follow your reading.** A new chat font size slider scales text from 80% to 120% of the note size, new chat tabs show titles by default, and light-theme provider icons have stronger contrast.
- **Dependencies are current.** The Claude Agent SDK is 0.3.322, and the transitive `hono` packages are patched so `pnpm audit --prod` is clean.

Existing conversations and saved preferences load unchanged.
